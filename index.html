How to Play 5×5 Bingo

  • 1. 5×5 Grid (1–25): Every player has numbers 1 to 25 arranged on a 5×5 board.
  • 2. Shared Calls: Players take turns tapping any uncrossed number (1–25). That number gets crossed off on all 4 boards simultaneously.
  • 3. Slash B-I-N-G-O: Each completed row, column, or diagonal (12 possible lines) slashes out one letter of B-I-N-G-O.
  • 4. Win Condition: First player(s) to slash all 5 letters of B-I-N-G-O wins!
  • 5. Tap Rivals Anytime: Tap any player card in the top strip to peek at their board (in Online rooms, rival boards stay hidden until B-I-N-G-O!).
  • 6. Online 4-Player Rooms: Switch mode to Online Room, create a 6-character Room Code, and share it with friends on any device.
